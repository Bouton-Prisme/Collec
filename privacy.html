Privacy Policy

We value your privacy. This store operates with minimal data collection:

  • No account creation required.
  • Essential session cookies protect checkout forms and administrator access.

  • We store your contact email, order details, transaction reference, optional message and order status on this site to process your order.
  • Your private tracking link provides access to your order details. Keep it confidential. Contact support for questions about access, correction or deletion of your data.
